Privacy Policy

Hiromu Kobayashi (the "Operator") sets out below how user information is handled in the iOS and Android app "Guitar Chord Book" (the "App").

1. Information you enter in the App

Information you enter in the App, such as chord fingerings and names, songs (titles, chord progressions and backing settings) and display settings, is stored only on your device. It is not sent to the Operator's or any third party's servers (information sent for advertising and analytics is described in sections 4 and 5). The App has no account registration or sign-in.

2. Microphone and recordings

The App uses the microphone only when you record your voice or an instrument over a backing track in the recording studio. The microphone is used only if you allow it, and you can change this at any time in your device's Settings. Recorded audio is stored only on your device and is not sent to the Operator's or any third party's servers.

3. Exporting and sharing audio

The feature that exports a song's audio to a file and shares it runs only when you tap the share button. You choose the destination (AirDrop, email, cloud storage and so on) in your device's share sheet, and handling beyond that point follows the terms of the app or service you choose.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays ads using Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC. To deliver ads and measure their performance, AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, advertising ID on Android), IP address, and device and app information. On iOS, the IDFA is used only if you allow tracking when asked; otherwise non-personalized ads are shown. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android, you can delete or reset your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads. In regions where consent is required, the App asks for your consent before showing ads. For details, see Google's policy. The chords, songs and recordings you create in the App are not sent for advertising.

5. Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics)

To understand which features are used and to improve the App, the App uses Firebase Analytics provided by Google LLC. What is sent is the type of action (saving a chord, saving a song, playing a song, starting a recording, exporting audio) together with information such as the app version and device type. It does not include information that identifies you personally. The contents of your chords, song titles and recordings are not sent. For how Google handles information, see the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Deleting information

You can delete chords, songs and recordings individually in the App. Deleting the App removes all information stored on your device. Because the Operator does not store the information you enter on any server, there is nothing for the Operator to delete. Information sent to Google for advertising and analytics is handled under Google's policies.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will announce it on this page.
