Guitar Chord Book Support

Guitar Chord Book lets you write down guitar chord fingerings, build songs from chord progressions, and play them back as a backing track.

Frequently asked questions

Q. Do I need an account?
A. No. Install the app and every feature is ready to use.

Q. How do I save a chord?
A. On the CHORDS tab, tap "New chord", then tap the fretboard where your fingers go. Use the ○ and × at the left edge to switch each string between open and muted.

Q. I don't know the chord's name.
A. Tap "Auto" in the chord name field and the app names the chord from your fingering. When the same notes can be read another way, you can pick from the candidates. "Auto" is disabled when the fingering can't be identified.

Q. How do I make a song?
A. On the SONGS tab, tap "New song". Choose a key and the chords you use most in that key line up; tap them to fill the bars. You can also start from a classic progression.

Q. Can I add drums and bass?
A. Yes. In the song editor's Style tab, choose a strumming style and turn on drums and bass, or the guitar solo. You can also change these per section.

Q. How do I change a song's key?
A. You can transpose the whole song from the song editor. Basic major and minor chords are transposed; other chords are left as they are.

Q. I can't record.
A. The app asks for microphone access the first time you record. If you declined, allow the microphone for this app in your device's Settings.

Q. The backing track ends up in my recording.
A. If the backing plays through the speaker, the microphone picks it up. Please wear earphones while recording.

Q. My recording is slightly out of time with the backing.
A. Depending on your device and earphones there can be a small offset. Use Timing on each track in the recording studio to move it earlier or later.

Q. Can I send a song to my computer or another app?
A. Yes. Use the share button at the top right of the song screen to export the backing and your recordings as one WAV file. WAV is uncompressed, so long songs make large files.

Q. Does it support Drop D or other tunings?
A. Not yet. The app currently supports standard tuning only.

Q. Are there ads?
A. A single banner ad is shown on the chord list and song list screens. There are no ads on the editing, playback or recording screens.

Q. Does it work offline?
A. Yes. Your chords, songs and recordings are stored on your device, and every feature works offline.

Q. Can I move my data to a new phone?
A. Chords, songs and recordings are stored only on your device, so the app has no transfer feature of its own.
