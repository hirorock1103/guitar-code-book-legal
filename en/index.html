Jot chords.
Build songs. Play.

Guitar Chord Book is an iPhone and Android app that combines a notebook for guitar chords with a songwriting tool that turns chord progressions into songs.

What you can do

Save your chords. Tap the on-screen fretboard where your fingers go. Set open and muted strings one by one. 14 basic major and minor chords are included from the start.

Check the sound and the name. Strum the exact fingering you entered. Tap "Auto" to have the app name the chord, and see its notes and the scales that fit.

Build a song from a progression. Choose a key and the six chords you use most in it line up. Make sections such as Intro, Verse and Chorus, then arrange them into a full song.

Add a backing band and record. Six strumming styles, with drums, bass and a guitar solo on top. Record your voice or your own guitar over the backing track, then export the mix as an audio file to share.
